What is Panchakarma?

Panchakarma is one of the most effective healing processes in Ayurveda. It is a detoxification and rejuvenation therapy designed to restore balance in the body and mind by removing deep-rooted toxins. This ancient therapy not only helps cleanse the digestive system but also strengthens immunity, improves metabolism, and enhances mental clarity.

At Shrikrishna Arogyadham, our experienced Ayurvedic doctors provide personalized Panchakarma treatments to address each patient’s unique health condition. Whether you’re facing stress, chronic pain, hormonal imbalance, or lifestyle fatigue — Panchakarma restores your natural harmony and inner peace.
